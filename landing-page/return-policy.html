Return Policy

Our return terms are designed to keep the process fair, transparent, and efficient.

What Returns/Exchanges Cover

  • Product faults
  • Sizing concerns
  • Wrong product shipment

Process

  • Customers arrange courier/reverse pickup for defective or wrong product
  • Free pickup depends on mutual agreement
  • If accepted, pickup request is raised within 7 working days
  • Exchange starts after product is received at warehouse

Time Window

  • 7-day return policy
  • 7-day replacement/exchange policy
  • Refund processing: 3-5 days; bank credit: 5-7 working days
