Smart AI Scanner

Our proprietary AI instantly breaks down complex ingredient lists into simple, easy-to-understand insights so you don’t have to decode scientific names or misleading claims on your own. Flean Score

No more guesswork. Every item receives a precise, easy-to-understand score from 1 to 10, thoughtfully calculated based on ingredient purity and nutritional density, not marketing claims or front-of-pack promises.

Behind this simple number is a deep analysis of what truly matters: the quality of ingredients, the presence of hidden additives, the use of harmful oils or excessive sugars, and the overall nutritional value your body actually benefits from.
