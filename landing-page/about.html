About Flean

Verified. Vetted. Delivered.

Since 2026, we've been making grocery shopping effortless by delivering thousands of packaged products to your home in just 30 minutes. We believe convenience shouldn't come at the cost of choice or quality.

What Drives Us

Our mission, vision, and values

Our mission, vision, and values shape every decision we make.

🎯

Our Mission

To make everyday grocery shopping effortless by providing instant access to thousands of quality packaged products. We're committed to delivering convenience and reliability with every order.

👁️

Our Vision

To become the most trusted quick-commerce platform globally, redefining how people shop for groceries through technology, speed, and unmatched product selection.

🛡️

Our Values

Customer obsession, operational excellence, innovation, and reliability drive everything we do. We believe in building trust through consistent, high-quality service.

Meet the Team

The People Behind Flean

Passionate individuals working together to revolutionize grocery delivery.

Rakshit Gupta

Full Stack Engineer

If you’ve interacted with Flean’s WhatsApp Assistant, you’ve experienced Rakshit’s work firsthand. He is a Full Stack Engineer who thrives on turning complex data orchestration into seamless, lightning-fast interactions. From crafting asynchronous backends to refining user-facing features, Rakshit ensures our AI layer runs flawlessly. Off-duty, he is a serial builder of passion projects (like custom browser IDEs) and is always looking for ways to automate the world around him.

Miral Songhela

Co-Founder & COO

Every visionary idea needs a master executioner, and that’s Miral. As Co-Founder and COO, he manages the beautiful, behind-the-scenes magic that keeps Flean scaling flawlessly. A proven builder, Miral previously co-founded Veeksha, a 3D experiential learning platform backed by major industry players and acquired by Adda247. Now, he’s steering the operational wheel at Flean to bring nutritional transparency to millions. When he’s not untangling a business challenge, he’s usually hunting for the next big technical puzzle to solve or cheering on his favorite passion projects.

Anuj Kumar

Co-Founder & CEO

Anuj builds things that touch millions of lives. From scaling household tech names like Paytm and Times Internet to driving engineering excellence as the CTO of Adda247, his career has been all about turning complex code into massive real-world impact. Now, he’s applying that exact same problem-solving energy to consumer food health with Flean. A tech veteran by trade and a deeply curious creator by nature, Anuj is just as likely to be found optimizing our backend recommendation systems as he is dominating the local badminton courts or sharing a laugh with his daughter, Amaya.
