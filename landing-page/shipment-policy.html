Shipment Policy

This page explains order processing, shipping charges, COD terms, and delivery timelines.

Order Processing

Orders placed Monday-Friday (excluding holidays) are processed in 2-3 business days after payment verification. Weekend/holiday orders start next business day.

Pre-order products may take up to 4 weeks to ship.

Delivery Timeline

Once shipped, delivery typically takes 5-7 working days.

Shipping Fees

  • Standard Shipping: Rs. 40 across India
  • Free Shipping: orders above Rs. 1000

Cash on Delivery (COD)

  • Rs. 100 for orders below Rs. 1000
  • Rs. 50 for orders above Rs. 1000
  • COD booking fee of Rs. 100 to be paid online

Other Information

  • All prices include GST
  • Tracking number is shared via email/SMS after dispatch
  • Delays may occur due to weather/holidays/unforeseen factors

International Shipping

At present, we are focused on shipping within India. For special shipping requests, contact customer service.
