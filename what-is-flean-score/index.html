Deep Dive

Protein isn't just
about grams

Most labels show you grams. We go deeper. Flean evaluates protein using scientific metrics that measure real nutritional value.

PDCAAS

Protein Digestibility
Corrected Amino Acid Score

Measures protein quality based on amino acid requirements and digestibility

DIAAS

Digestible Indispensable
Amino Acid Score

A more accurate, modern successor to PDCAAS

BV

Bioavailability

How much protein your body can actually absorb and use

We penalise false
claims

Brands that make misleading claims on their packaging like "high protein" without adequate protein density, or "sugar-free" with hidden sweeteners receive additional penalties in their Flean Score.
